[New Features]
- Bluetooth LE Audio: enabled the LE Audio unicast client (along with CSIP,
  VCP, MCP, and CCP) and broadcast assistant. LE Audio headphones work
  directly and can join Auracast broadcasts; LC3 encoding runs via
  system software. Dual-mode headphones still default to A2DP/HFP; enable

- Fixed a null pointer crash when, with a mouse and keyboard connected,
  pressing the left mouse button on an empty area of a page holding a pinned

[Platform]

== 中文 ==

[修复]
- 修复 Wi-Fi 关闭后无法重新开启的问题
  （感谢酷安用户 /u/1861882 反馈，定位与修复来自其提供的分析）
- 修复官方手写笔书写无反应：面板在 144 Hz 下收不到 NVT 手写笔输入。
  现在连接官方 Lenovo 手写笔（蓝牙）时自动将最高刷新率限制为 120 Hz。

[新功能]
- 充电控制改用原厂同款实现：健康 HAL 直接写入原厂充电驱动
  （保护设置节点）的充电上限，行为与原厂「最大充电上限」一致，
  上限 80% 时在 76% 恢复充电。可在 设置 -> 电池 -> 充电控制 使用，
  并支持定时充电。
- 旁路供电：在「性能与内存」中为指定应用开启后，应用在前台且已连接充电器时
  电池停止充电、由适配器直接供电；电量降至 20% 时自动恢复并提醒。
  已安装 GameSpace 时，也可对正在游玩的游戏自动启用。
- 充电高温保护：充电时电量不低于 40% 且电池温度达到阈值
  （39 / 41 / 43 ℃，默认 41 ℃）时暂停充电，温度下降后自动恢复，
  以减缓高温充电对电池的损耗。

[兼容性]
- 同步上游最新的 MediaTek GED KPI 支持（frameworks/native），
  不再使用本地 frameworks/native 分支。GPU 可通过帧时间戳跟随帧截止时间
  调频，改善高负载下 GPU 频率停在 265-400 MHz 造成的丢帧。

== English ==

[Fixes]
- Fixed Wi-Fi not turning back on after being turned off
  (reported by CoolAPK user /u/1861882; the cause and the fix come from the
  analysis they provided)
- Fixed the official stylus drawing and writing nothing: the panel reports no
  NVT pen input at 144 Hz. The maximum refresh rate is now capped at 120 Hz
  automatically while an official Lenovo pen is connected over Bluetooth.

[New Features]
- Charging control now uses the stock implementation: the health HAL writes
  the charging limit of the stock charger driver (the protection settings
  node) directly, behaving like stock's "Maximum charge limit", resuming
  charging at 76% for an 80% limit. Available in Settings -> Battery ->
  Charging control, including scheduled charging.
- Bypass charging: enabled per app under "Performance and memory", it stops
  battery charging while the app is in front and the charger is connected, so
  the adapter powers the tablet directly; charging resumes at 20% battery
  with a warning. With GameSpace installed, it can also be enabled
  automatically for the running game.
- Charging heat protection: while charging at 40% battery or above, charging
  pauses once the battery temperature reaches the threshold
  (39 / 41 / 43 °C, 41 °C by default) and resumes once it cools, reducing
  battery wear from high-temperature charging.

[Compatibility]
- Synced upstream's latest MediaTek GED KPI support (frameworks/native) and
  dropped the local frameworks/native fork. GPU DVFS follows frame deadlines
  from frame timestamps, improving dropped frames caused by GPU clocks
  stalling at 265-400 MHz under full load.
